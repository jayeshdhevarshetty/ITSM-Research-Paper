# IT Service Management Research Paper

This is my research paper about how IT teams deal with problems when systems are not working correctly. I focused on incident, problem, and change management because these are important parts of keeping IT systems running.

One thing I learned is that just closing a ticket is not always enough. If the same issue keeps coming back, the team should find out why it is happening and make a proper fix. The paper explains how these three processes can work together instead of being handled separately.

The full paper covers three important areas in IT service management.

- Incident Management helps bring a service back when something goes wrong.
- Problem Management helps find the real reason an issue keeps happening.
- Change Management helps teams make system changes in a safe way.

## What this paper is about

Modern businesses depend on IT systems every day. When an application, server, or database fails, it can slow down work and affect customers. This paper looks at how IT teams can handle these issues in a better way.

Instead of only fixing the same issue again and again, IT teams can do the following.

1. Restore the service quickly.
2. Find the root cause of repeated issues.
3. Make a safe, long-term fix.
4. Monitor the system to make sure the problem does not come back.

## Why this matters

Good IT service management helps companies in many ways.

- Reduce system downtime.
- Solve important issues faster.
- Avoid repeating the same work.
- Improve communication between teams.
- Make changes with less risk.
